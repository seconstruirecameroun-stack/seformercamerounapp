Fil conducteur : One word describes another, and sometimes it keeps a capital letter.

## Narration

1. An adjective is a word that describes a noun.
2. Heavier is not a sentence. The hammer is heavy — that is a sentence.
3. Common adjectives take no capital letter.
4. Proper adjectives come from places, and they keep their capital letter.
